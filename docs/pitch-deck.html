SLIDE 04 / 10THE PRODUCT

Six worker profiles. One unit price each.

  • Customer Support · SDR · Researcher · Writer · Ops Coordinator · QA Auditor.
  • Each profile carries a unit price ($5–$12 / cleared outcome) and a verification rule.
  • The runtime reads your source of truth at run time. No fine-tuning.
  • Per-shift receipts list cleared lines and voided lines.
  • The receipt is the contract.
SLIDE 05 / 10HOW IT WORKS

How a shift gets paid out.

  • 1. Worker runs against the queue.
  • 2. Each line is verified against a buyer-side rule.
  • 3. Cleared lines draw down the pool. Voided lines never charge.
  • 4. Receipt is issued weekly.
  • 5. Settlement = NOWPayments USDT/USDC; invoice is USD-denominated.
