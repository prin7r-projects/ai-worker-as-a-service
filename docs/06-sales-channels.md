# 06 — Sales channels: Tally

The audience map in doc 05 lands directly on this channel mix. Each channel is here because a named persona is already there.

## Channel A — Operator-leader Slack & community networks (Maya)

**Where:** Pavilion, Operations Nation, RevGenius, OpsStars, CHIEF, Heads of CS / Heads of Sales Ops sub-Slacks.

**Why it fits.** Maya is in 2–3 of these and treats them as her vendor-vetting layer. Posts that work: a short receipt-screenshot ("Last week: 312 of 350 tickets resolved, $1,872 charged. No retainer. No tokens. Here's the verification rule we used.") + a link to the landing.

**Activation pattern.**
1. Member-of-record (a Tally team principal) posts a *one-receipt-a-week* ledger entry into 2–3 communities, no link in the first post.
2. When asked "what is this?", reply with the landing URL.
3. Convert: members DM for a Trial deposit; Trial converts at 25–35 % to Standard within 30 days.

**Cost.** $0 marginal. Membership fees for Pavilion ($2K/y) and CHIEF ($8K/y) are sunk.

## Channel B — LinkedIn organic + thought-leadership (Maya & Devon)

**Where:** LinkedIn home feed, COO/VP newsletters, "operator note" newsletters (Lenny's Newsletter referrals).

**Why it fits.** Maya skims LinkedIn daily; Devon does too but engages more. The receipt-screenshot motif works better on LinkedIn than on X because the audience is older and reads.

**Activation pattern.**
- One Tally founder + two operator-friends post 3× / week each: receipts, retrospectives, "we changed the verification rule and recovered $X". Comment on COO posts.
- A monthly **outcome-economy newsletter** ("This month's cleared lines"), shipped to subscribers via Beehiiv. Free.

**Cost.** Time. ~10 hr / week between the founder and contracted operator-friends.

## Channel C — Indie Hackers / agency-owner Slacks (Devon)

**Where:** Indie Hackers home feed, Agency Mavericks Slack, NoCode Brothers, Bonjoro user community.

**Why it fits.** Devon's primary watering hole. He buys via DM after seeing 1–2 receipt posts; he doesn't read whitepapers.

**Activation pattern.**
- Tally posts: "We're letting agencies resell shifts at 30 % markup. Receipt PDF carries your logo. Reply if you want a partner agreement." Run quarterly.
- Devon-shaped buyers convert via the Trial tier on the landing (no sales call), then escalate to partner program.

**Cost.** $0 marginal.

## Channel D — Inbound from comparison content (procurement & Maya)

**Where:** SEO landing pages targeted at "alternative to manus", "alternative to lindy", "outcome priced ai agents", "ai support agent unit pricing", "ai sdr per outcome".

**Why it fits.** Procurement and Maya both google these terms when filling a vendor matrix. Tally is structurally different from Manus / Lindy / Devin and that difference is the main story.

**Activation pattern.**
- Ship 8 comparison pages over Q1: `/vs/manus`, `/vs/lindy`, `/vs/devin`, `/vs/cognition`, `/vs/cleric`, `/vs/sierra-ai`, plus 2 educational posts ("How outcome-billed AI is priced", "What an AI worker receipt should contain").
- Each page links to the Tier Pricing block on the landing.

**Cost.** ~$2K of writing time over Q1 (one editor). No paid SEO tooling.

## Channel E — Conference 1:1s (Maya & procurement)

**Where:** SaaStr Annual, HumanX, INBOUND, Lenny's offsites.

**Why it fits.** This is where Maya's signing-power decisions form. A 7-minute conversation at a booth is worth a 10-day inbound funnel.

**Activation pattern.**
- No booth in Year 1. Tally founders attend two events per quarter, work the hallway, and walk away with 5–10 receipt-Trial buyers each. Each Trial buyer is followed up 2 weeks later.
- Year 2: a small booth at SaaStr only, designed as a literal receipt printer.

**Cost.** Travel + ticket — ~$3K per event. Two events / quarter = $24K / year ceiling.

## Channel F — Vendor newsletters / aggregators (procurement)

**Where:** Hooked.so style operator-tool roundups, "AI agent" comparison sites, Operations Nation tool guides.

**Why it fits.** Procurement decision-makers subscribe to these.

**Activation pattern.** Outreach for inclusion (free) once the Standard tier has 25+ paying customers. Tally is the only outcome-priced entry, which is the lede of the post.

**Cost.** $0.

## Channels we deliberately are **not** running

- **Paid social.** Maya does not click ads for ops tools; Devon does not either.
- **Twitter/X organic.** The audience is not there in volume; the X bias toward "agentic" hype hurts our positioning.
- **Cold email at scale.** Our primary persona is exhausted by AI cold outbound and would screen Tally as another offender.
- **Affiliate networks.** We have a partner program (Devon-shaped). Generic affiliates are not aligned with the receipt-quality bar.
- **Reddit.** Not where Maya, Devon, or procurement transact.

## Channel mix in plain English

In Q1: communities (A) + LinkedIn (B) drive Trial signups; comparison content (D) drives procurement + Maya inbound. Conference 1:1s (E) at SaaStr Annual seed Standard-tier customers. Devon-shaped buyers (C) come in at low volume but high LTV via the partner program.

The success metric per channel is **first cleared receipt within 14 days of first touch** — not a click, not a signup. A buyer who downloads a comparison page but never has a cleared line item is not a converted customer.
