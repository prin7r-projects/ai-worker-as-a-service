# Slide 4 — The product

- A **catalog of pre-trained worker profiles**: Customer Support, SDR, Researcher, Writer, Ops Coordinator, QA Auditor.
- A **unit-priced outcome contract** per profile ($5–$12 per cleared outcome).
- A **runtime** that runs the worker against the buyer's source of truth at run time.
- A **per-shift receipt** that lists cleared lines and voided lines.
- The receipt is the contract.

---

# Slide 5 — How a shift gets paid out

- 1. Worker runs against the queue (no fine-tuning; reads source of truth at run time).
- 2. Each line is verified against a **buyer-side rule** (CS = customer-set resolution status; SDR = personalized message + reply or non-bounce; etc.).
- 3. Cleared lines draw down the buyer's pool. Voided lines are voided on the receipt and never charged.
- 4. The receipt is issued at end-of-shift (weekly).
- 5. Settlement = NOWPayments USDT/USDC; invoice is USD-denominated.
